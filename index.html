02 — About

Designing products

I’m Arna — a UX/UI designer with a background in information systems. I work across internal platforms, marketplaces and mobile products, connecting interface design with the logic behind it.

My experience includes translating requirements into user flows, designing forms and multistep processes, and organising tables, statuses, filters and permissions.

I collaborate with product managers and developers, prepare designs for implementation and review the finished interfaces. I work with existing UI kits and design systems to keep products consistent.

From requirements to the final interface — I care about how the whole product works.
